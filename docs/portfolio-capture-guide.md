# Portfolio Capture Guide

Use real running-app captures only. Do not use mock screenshots for the portfolio.

## Recommended screenshots

Capture these at a consistent phone viewport:

1. **Interactive demo onboarding** — include the demo CTA and local-first explanation.
2. **Today command centre** — show realistic sample scores, the three next moves and quick actions.
3. **Adaptive revision scheduler** — show subjects with mixed confidence and the generated allocation.
4. **Habit workflow** — show several habits, completion state and a visible streak.
5. **Analytics** — show trend charts and calendar-based consistency.
6. **Demo controls / recoverability** — show reset demo and restore-previous-data controls.

For the GitHub README, the strongest three are Today, Revision Scheduler and Analytics.

## 60–90 second video

A strong recruiter walkthrough should:

- open the interactive demo instead of typing setup data;
- spend the first 10 seconds on the Today command centre;
- open the revision scheduler and change one subject from high to low confidence so the allocation visibly changes;
- tick a habit and briefly show the analytics/review loop;
- finish in Settings by showing that demo data can be reset or exited without overwriting the prior workspace.

Keep the recording focused on product behavior rather than scrolling through every setting.

## Suggested captions

- **Today:** “Ranks the next useful actions from persisted goals and habit state.”
- **Revision:** “Confidence- and deadline-aware scheduling engine with PDF export.”
- **Analytics:** “Calendar-window metrics avoid stale or duplicate record inflation.”
- **Recovery:** “Validated backup restore with rollback protection and isolated demo data.”
